# Mis Pagos — Product Thesis

## Status
High-level product-positioning contract.

This document exists to prevent Mis Pagos from drifting into a generic budget tracker, agenda, banking dashboard or feature-heavy personal-finance app.

## 1. Core problem
People often know how much money is visible in an account or wallet, but that number does not answer:

> ¿Cuánto de esto realmente puedo usar?

Part of that money may already be committed to rent, utilities, subscriptions, installments, loans or other known future obligations.

The gap between visible balance and usable money creates uncertainty.

## 2. Core promise
Mis Pagos should answer quickly:

> ¿Cuánto dinero tengo comprometido y cuánto me queda realmente disponible?

Then help the user decide:

> ¿Qué puedo hacer con ese dinero sin perjudicar lo que ya sé que viene?

## 3. Product position
Mis Pagos is not primarily:
- a bank;
- an expense-history tracker;
- a spreadsheet replacement;
- a budgeting methodology;
- an accounting system;
- an AI financial assistant;
- a generic agenda.

Its focus is:

> future commitments + current balance + personal intent → useful decision today

## 4. Anti-budget principle
The product should remain useful without:
- bank connections;
- transaction imports;
- full categorization;
- receipt scanning;
- exhaustive expense history;
- complex budget setup.

The user provides only what materially improves the future picture.

## 5. Forward-looking orientation
Traditional trackers often answer:

> ¿En qué gasté?

Mis Pagos should primarily answer:

> ¿Qué viene?

and:

> ¿Qué puedo hacer ahora?

Historical information is secondary to decision support.

## 6. Core product ladder
### Level 1 — Disponible real
What remains after known commitments?

### Level 2 — Planning
What portion do I want to preserve and how do I want to distribute the rest?

### Level 3 — Decision support
What happens if I spend X today?

### Level 4 — Forecasting
At my current pace, how am I likely to finish the period?

This ladder should guide roadmap decisions.

## 7. Why not Notion or an agenda?
A general-purpose tool may technically reproduce the logic.

Mis Pagos should remove the work of designing the system.

The user should not need to:
- create databases;
- build formulas;
- configure templates;
- maintain a custom spreadsheet;
- decide what information matters.

The product should produce the answer directly.

## 8. Five-second test
A user should be able to open the app and quickly understand:
- current money;
- committed money;
- Disponible real;
- what needs attention.

As planning evolves:
- reserve;
- Libre para usar;
- safe pace.

Do not sacrifice this for feature density.

## 9. Differentiation stack
Mis Pagos becomes more distinctive through the combination of:
- local-first financial data;
- no mandatory bank connection;
- minimal setup;
- future-first commitments;
- Disponible real;
- reserve/planning layer;
- `¿Puedo gastar esto?` simulation;
- LATAM-friendly currencies;
- optional reference currencies;
- personal visual identity;
- non-punitive emotional progression.

No single feature must be globally unique.

The combination and focus are the differentiation.

## 10. Emotional identity
Financial organization should feel:
- relieving;
- clear;
- calm;
- personal;
- worth returning to.

`Tu espacio`, personalization and achievements make useful organization emotionally memorable, but they are not the financial engine.

## 11. Product sentence
Working internal thesis:

> No te decimos en qué gastaste tu dinero. Te ayudamos a decidir qué podés hacer con el que todavía tenés.

Alternative operational formulation:

> Mis Pagos te dice cuánto de tu dinero realmente podés usar, teniendo en cuenta lo que ya sabés que viene.

These are product-direction statements, not mandatory marketing copy.

## 12. What earns Home space
A feature earns Home space only if it helps answer one of these:
1. What do I have?
2. What is committed?
3. What is truly available?
4. What needs attention?
5. What can I safely do next?

Everything else should justify secondary placement.

## 13. Product guardrails
Avoid drifting toward:
- generic dashboards;
- excessive charts;
- exhaustive categories;
- feature checklists copied from competitors;
- AI for its own sake;
- banking complexity;
- social gamification;
- investment tracking;
- full accounting;
- cluttered Home screens.

## 14. Roadmap filter
Before building a major feature ask:
1. Does this improve financial truth?
2. Does this improve planning?
3. Does this improve a decision?
4. Does this reduce maintenance burden?
5. Does this improve ownership/retention without harming clarity?

If none apply, priority should be low.

## 15. Success definition
The product succeeds if a user can say:

> Antes veía mi saldo. Ahora sé cuánto realmente puedo tocar.

And later:

> Antes de gastar, puedo ver qué efecto tendría sobre mi mes.

That is the behavior Mis Pagos should own.
